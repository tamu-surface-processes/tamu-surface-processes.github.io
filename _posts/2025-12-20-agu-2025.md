Texas A&M Surface processes presented *five abstracts at AGU 2025 this year*!
That includes two presentations from students in the group, Zhilin Shi and Lena Timko.

Lena and Zhilin both gave poster presentations about their in-progress Master's research, and got to meet and discuss with many of the scientists that have inspired their work.
